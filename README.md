## Nicolas Brosse

**AI research & development — from mathematical foundations to working systems.**

I am a postdoctoral researcher at [CREST](https://crest.science/) with Arnak Dalalyan, working on two things:

- **The mathematics of generative models** — diffusion models and flow matching, how their samplers propagate errors, and what happens in the small-noise regime.
- **Human–AI collaboration in mathematical research** — [Human–AI Mathematics](https://human-ai-mathematics.github.io/), an open framework in which AI agents search for and cross-review proofs that mathematicians can check. Its first case study is the [KLS conjecture](https://github.com/human-ai-mathematics/kls-conjecture-search).

Before that: a PhD in statistics on Langevin sampling, then six years building deep learning systems in industry — signal processing at Thales, drug discovery at Iktos, foundation models for biology at MBZUAI and GenBio AI — and LLM systems as a freelancer. What I care about is taking an idea from paper to working system, and measuring whether it actually works.
